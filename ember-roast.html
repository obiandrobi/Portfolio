Our story

Ember Roast started as a garage roaster, a farmers-market stand, and a stubborn belief that Riverside deserved a coffee shop that felt like a deep breath.

In 2019, Maya and Jordan Park started roasting beans in their garage off Magnolia Avenue, selling bags off a folding table at the Saturday market. The line kept getting longer. The folding table became a food truck; the food truck became this little sunlit shop on Main Street.

Six years on, the beans still get roasted in small batches, the regulars still have "their" seats, and the chalkboard by the door still gets a new terrible coffee pun every Monday. Some things should never change.

Come for the coffee. Stay because nobody here is in a hurry to make you leave.
