Poof

Portfolio Piece #2 — Animated App Home Screen

Poof is a cozy daily-quest companion app. Poof is a shy little monster who lives in a cave in the hills, longing to visit the glowing town below. Every morning he greets you from his cave, and the tiny quests you finish together — a dance rescue, a followed ember, a cool rock that needs a name — grow the living flame on his head. Small steps, for both of you.

  • Animated cave-home header, generated from an original character design
  • Streak counter built on Poof's flame tuft — it grows as you complete quests
  • Working quest cards, progress ring, and ember rewards
  • Eight active quests — reflexes, memory, timing, a real-world rock, and one very fashionable rock
  • High scores for every game, saved on the device — replay quests to beat your best
  • Finish all eight quests and Poof celebrates
  • Four working tabs, including the Map — the town below, someday
Try it: tap the quest cards in the phone — all eight are active quests: cool dancing Poof down, follow the ember, name a cool rock for Steve, test your flame memory, wake the cave crystals, catch embers in a jar, pop smoke puffs, and give Steve a makeover (yes, there's a selfie). Scored games keep a high score, and finished quests can be replayed any time to beat it. Finish all eight for Poof's celebration.

✨ Follow the ember

How to play: a loose ember will drift around the flame outline.
1️⃣ Press Start, then press and hold on the canvas.
2️⃣ Keep your finger (or mouse) right on the glowing ember as it travels.
3️⃣ Stay on it for at least 70% of the trip to light Poof's flame!
