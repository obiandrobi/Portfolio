Poof

Portfolio Piece #2 — Animated App Home Screen

Poof is a cozy daily-quest companion app. Poof is a shy little monster who lives in a cave in the hills, longing to visit the glowing town below. Every morning he greets you from his cave, and the tiny quests you finish together — a stretch, a doodle, a glass of water — grow the living flame on his head. Small steps, for both of you.

  • Animated cave-home header, generated from an original character design
  • Streak counter built on Poof's flame tuft — it grows as you complete quests
  • Working quest cards, progress ring, and ember rewards
  • Finish all three quests and Poof celebrates
  • Four working tabs, including the Map — the town below, someday
Try it: tap the quest cards in the phone to check them off. Finish all three for Poof's celebration.
