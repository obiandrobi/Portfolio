Poof

Portfolio Piece #2 — Animated App Home Screen

Poof is a cozy daily-quest companion app. Poof is a shy little monster who lives in a cave in the hills, longing to visit the glowing town below. Every morning he greets you from his cave, and the tiny quests you finish together — a dance rescue, a traced flame, a cool rock that needs a name — grow the living flame on his head. Small steps, for both of you.

  • Animated cave-home header, generated from an original character design
  • Streak counter built on Poof's flame tuft — it grows as you complete quests
  • Working quest cards, progress ring, and ember rewards
  • Finish all three quests and Poof celebrates
  • Four working tabs, including the Map — the town below, someday
Try it: tap the quest cards in the phone — all three are active quests now: cool dancing Poof down by tapping the extra flames off his fur (don't let him overheat and fall asleep!), trace the ember flame well enough to light it, and go find a real cool rock, name it, and introduce it to Poof's rock, Steve. Finish all three for Poof's celebration.
