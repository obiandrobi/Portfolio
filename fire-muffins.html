Fire Muffin Catch

Portfolio Piece #3 — A Playable Poof Mini-Moment

Disaster in the hills: **fire muffins** are raining from the sky. They're Poof's favorite — warm, sweet, and slightly on fire — but he's still Poof, so catching them takes all the bravery he has. Which, today, is just enough.

  • A real playable game loop: move, catch, miss, score, hearts, win and lose states
  • Drag, mouse, or arrow keys — Poof follows with a little hop on every catch
  • Difficulty ramps: the muffins fall faster the more you catch
  • Catch 15 to win. Drop 3 and the muffins win
  • Sound effects throughout, chopped from ElevenLabs generations
How to play: press Start, then drag (or move your mouse) to slide Poof left and right. Catch the falling fire muffins — don't let them splat.
